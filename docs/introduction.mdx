Fold Health helps Transitional Care Organizations (TCOs), value-based care groups, and Medicare Advantage plans run every day of care work from a single screen: patient outreach, risk-adjustment (HCC) coding, HEDIS gap closure, chronic care management, campaigns, forms, and analytics — with AI voice and chat agents doing the repetitive parts.

## How the app is laid out

Fold Health has three navigation surfaces stacked left-to-right:

1. **Sidebar** (leftmost, ~64px collapsed) — Home, Population, Calendar, Tasks, Messages, Calls, Campaign, Analytics, Settings.
2. **SubNav** (only visible inside Population, ~200px) — the list of worklists (TOC, HCC, HEDIS, CCM, SNP, Annual Visit, JSA, DM, High Utilizers), plus **My Patients**, **All Patients**, **Population Groups**, and **Archived Worklists**.
3. **Content area** — the active worklist table, a patient profile, an analytics dashboard, or a full-screen builder.

Every URL is hash-based (`#/population/hcc`, `#/analytics/risk`, `#/settings/agents`) — deep links to any patient, tab, drawer, or dashboard are shareable.

## Where to start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install and run">
    See [Installation](/getting-started/installation) — the app runs with **Bun** at `http://localhost:5173`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the default view">
    You land on the **TOC Worklist** (`#/population/toc`). Every other worklist is one click away in the SubNav.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow a worklist walkthrough">
    Pick a workflow you actually run: [TOC](/features/toc-worklist), [HCC](/features/hcc-worklist), [HEDIS](/features/hedis-worklist), [CCM](/features/ccm-worklist), or [AWV](/features/awv-worklist).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure an agent">
    Open **Settings → Agents**, pick an agent, and edit its flow in [Agent Builder](/features/agent-builder).
  </Step>
</Steps>
